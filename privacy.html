Privacy Policy for Alika AI

Effective Date: September 2026

Alika AI ("we", "our", or "the application") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled across our Windows desktop application and official website.

1. Microphone & Voice Data

Alika AI accesses your device's microphone exclusively when actively in session to process voice interactions in real time. Voice audio streams are transmitted via encrypted WebSockets directly to the Google Gemini Live API for processing and are discarded immediately. We do not record, store, log, or sell your voice data.

2. System Controls & Tools

Desktop features such as media playback controls (play, pause, next, volume) and app executions run strictly on your local device using native Windows APIs. The application does not monitor your private files, keystrokes, personal media, or web browsing activity.

3. Data Collection & Analytics

Our desktop application does not collect personally identifiable information (PII). Our landing page uses standard Google Analytics (GA4) exclusively for aggregated traffic statistics.

4. Third-Party Services

Voice interactions utilize the Google Gemini Live API subject to enterprise encryption and standard data privacy standards.

5. Contact & Support

For questions or support regarding this Privacy Policy, visit our official page: https://alika-ai.vercel.app/.
