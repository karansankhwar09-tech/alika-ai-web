Privacy & Security Policy

Version 1.0.4 • Effective: November 2026

ALIKA AI ("we", "our", or "the application") respects your personal privacy and values architectural transparency. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled across our Windows desktop application and official website.

Local-First Guarantee: ALIKA AI operates on a private Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) architecture. Your Gemini API key is stored locally on your machine in secrets.json. No intermediate developer servers intercept, log, or broker your communications.

1. Microphone & Voice Data

ALIKA AI Desktop accesses your device's microphone exclusively when actively in session or when the optional Hands-Free "Hey Alika" Wake Word listener is enabled. Voice audio streams are transmitted via TLS-encrypted WebSockets directly to Google Gemini Live WebSockets for real-time natural language reasoning. We do not record, store, log, or sell your voice data to any third party.

2. Screen Perception & RAM Frames

When you explicitly trigger Alt + Q ("Ask About Screen") or activate screen-reading tools, ALIKA AI captures an in-memory frame of your desktop via local Win32/MSS APIs. These frames reside strictly in volatile RAM for the duration of the query and are discarded immediately upon completion. ALIKA AI does not continuously broadcast, stream, or archive your screen.

3. Local Data Storage & SQLite Memory

Personal memories, study targets, and daily progress are persisted locally on your device within a dedicated SQLite database (alika_daily_memory.sqlite) and local JSON configs. You retain complete ownership and physical control over these files. They can be inspected or purged at any time by deleting the local application directory.

4. System Automation & Windows APIs

Desktop automation actions—such as brightness adjustments, audio volume controls, media controls (GSMTC), and application launching—execute on your local machine via our lightweight local FastAPI agent running on port 8765. The agent interacts with authorized Windows UI Automation (UIA) APIs only upon user instruction and does not monitor private keystrokes or unrelated filesystem directories.

5. Website Privacy, Cookies & Discussions

The official website (https://alika-ai.vercel.app/) does not deploy third-party advertising cookies or tracking beacons. Client-side storage (localStorage) is used solely to store local feedback sandbox entries and UI preferences (e.g. Calm Mode). Community discussions are powered by GitHub Discussions via Giscus, which operates subject to GitHub's standard privacy practices.

6. Third-Party Services & Google Gemini

Direct API communications connect directly from your machine to Google Cloud AI endpoints using your personal Google AI Studio key. Usage is governed by Google Cloud and Google AI Studio API Terms of Service.

7. Contact & Security Inquiries

For questions or security disclosures regarding this policy, reach out via our official GitHub repository: karansankhwar09-tech/alika-ai-web.
